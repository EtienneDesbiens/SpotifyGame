Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-12

Hear-ster ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial project that lets you play music-guessing games using your own Spotify library. This page explains what data the app accesses and what happens to it.

What the app accesses

When you log in with Spotify, the app requests access to:

  • Your basic profile (display name, account email, profile image)
  • Your playlists, liked songs, saved albums, top tracks, recently played tracks, and followed artists — to build the track lists you play games with
  • Playback control — to play song snippets through Spotify's Web Playback SDK during games

What the app does with it

All of the above is used only to run the game in your current session. The app does not have a database and does not permanently store your Spotify data, your listening activity, or your game results on any server. Game state lives in your browser/app for the duration of a session and is discarded when it ends.

Your Spotify access and refresh tokens are stored locally on your own device (browser localStorage on web, app storage on Android) so you don't have to log in every time. They are never sent anywhere other than Spotify's own API and this app's backend, which uses them solely to make API calls on your behalf.

What the app does not do

  • It does not sell, share, or otherwise disclose your data to any third party
  • It does not use your data for advertising or analytics
  • It does not store your data after your session ends

Third-party services

The app relies on the Spotify Web API and Web Playback SDK to function. Your use of Spotify through this app is also subject to Spotify's own Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

You can revoke this app's access to your Spotify account at any time from your Spotify account app permissions page.
